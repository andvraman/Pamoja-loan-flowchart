Financing Agreement · Schedule 2 · Sub-Component 1.2 (Mainland)

The CMG funding nest

Every solid arrow, tag and term on this page is taken from the Financing Agreement between the United Republic of Tanzania and the International Development Association, with the clause cited. Where the agreement does not say something, this page says not stated. The numbered dashed arrows are the one exception: they apply the usual lending meaning of “amortization schedule”, and are explained in the key beside the chart.

Dashed arrows 1–3: repayment under an amortization schedule. These apply the usual lending meaning of “amortization schedule”: the borrower repays the lender it entered into the schedule with. The agreement itself does not state who is repaid.
  1. 1Entrepreneur → CMG. The Microfinance Agreement specifies the amortization schedule of the Microfinancing resources lent to the beneficiary entrepreneur (I.D.6(a)). The CMG enters into that agreement (I.D.6) and provides the Microfinancing (Appendix).
  2. 2CMG → DIT. The Deposit Agreement specifies the amortization schedule of the resources to be on-lent to the beneficiary CMG (I.D.4(a)). Under I.D.4, the DIT enters into the Deposit Agreement with the CMG. The Deposit Agreement tag on the chart follows I.D.4.
  3. 3CMG → Recipient, through PO-RALG. The same schedule, read with the Appendix, which says the Deposit Agreement is entered into by the Recipient, through PO-RALG, and a CMG and the Deposited Amount is provided by the Recipient, through PO-RALG.

2 and 3 are alternatives. Which one applies is to be specified in the COM. The COM must set out the terms and conditions (i.e. single borrower limits, repayment schedules, charges, etc.) for the Deposited Amounts to be provided as capital injection to the CMGs (I.D.1(c)) and the proposed arrangement for the flow of funds from the Recipient to the CMGs, including the template of the Deposit Agreement (I.D.1(d)).

Payments back where the recipient is not stated

The Deposited Amount is on-lent to the beneficiary CMG (I.D.4(a)) and the Microfinancing is lent to the beneficiary entrepreneur (I.D.6(a)). Both are in the table above. The agreement also requires an amortization schedule for each, and gives a right to declare each immediately due and payable. It does not say to whom payments under the schedule, or amounts declared due, are made. The schedules are drawn on the chart as dashed arrows 1–3 (see key). Amounts declared due are not drawn.

Defined terms

Quoted from the Appendix.

Community Operations Manual (COM)
the Recipient’s operations manual to be developed and adopted pursuant to Section I.D.1 of Schedule 2 to this Agreement, for purpose of implementing Sub-Component 1.2 of the Project in Mainland.
Deposit Agreement
the on-lending agreement (to be) entered into by the Recipient, through PO-RALG, and a CMG, pursuant to Section I.E.4 of Schedule 2 to this Agreement, as well as the provisions of the COM, for the further on-lending to [a] member woman/en entrepreneur(s) for the implementation of eligible investment Subprojects.
Deposited Amount
each subordinated financing (to be) provided by the Recipient, through PO-RALG, to a CMG for purposes of injecting new capital thereto, pursuant to Section I.D.4 of Schedule 2 to this Agreement, as well as the provisions of Community Operations Manual. “Subordinated” is not defined in the agreement.
Microfinancing
each financing (to be) provided by a CMG to one or more member woman/en entrepreneur(s) for the implementation of an eligible investment Subproject pursuant to Section I.D.6 of Schedule 2 to this Agreement, as well as the provisions of Community Operations Manual.
Microfinance Agreement
the financing agreement (to be) entered into by a CMG with one or more member woman/en entrepreneur(s) pursuant to Section I.D.6 of Schedule 2 to this Agreement, as well as the provisions of the COM, for the provision of a Microfinancing.
Subproject
a productive investment to be implemented by [a] woman/en entrepreneur(s) which shall be/have been appraised and selected by a CMG or a PFI for financing under a Microfinancing or PFI Loan, as the case may be, pursuant to the protocols and criteria set forth in the COM and/or the PFI-OM
LGA
a Recipient’s local government authority established and operating (as the case may be) under either: (a) the Recipient’s Local Government (District Authorities) Act No 7 of 1982, as amended; or (b) the Recipient’s Local Government (Urban Authorities) Act. No. 8 of 1982, as amended.
Closing Date
The Closing Date is February 28, 2029. (Sch. 2, III.B.2)
