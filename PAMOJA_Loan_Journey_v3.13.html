Money map: who pays whom

CMGs sit at the centre. The LGA (30%) and the lender (70%) form the next ring. TAMISEMI is the outer arc. Green arrows are money going out; purple arrows are money going back to TAMISEMI; dashed pink arrows are the two possible routes for CMG incentives, not yet agreed (Q14). Key rules at a glance

Group size10 to 50 members, with an adopted constitution.
Track recordSavings or profits for at least one earlier cycle.
Lenders per council2 to 3, assigned by TAMISEMI.
Lenders per group1 to 2 TAMISEMI designated lenders, assigned by the LGA. Groups apply only to their assigned lenders.
AppraisalUses the criteria the lender gave TAMISEMI at selection.
InterestAt or below TAMISEMI’s ceiling.
AmountThe lender may approve less than requested.
RejectionsEvery rejection needs a clear reason.
EligibilityTAMISEMI sets national minimum criteria. Each LGA may add its own. The criteria (such as age and savings) are still to be agreed (Q16).
Offer and contractEvery offer comes with a loan contract and simplified key disclosures, including loan utilisation restrictions.
ChoiceThe group may accept or refuse each loan. Accepting needs a meeting with a quorum, and means the group has understood the terms and key disclosures.
SigningThe group signs the loan contract with the lender before any money is paid out.
After a defaultThe group may not be eligible for a new loan for a set period (Q18).
DisbursementWithin 15 working days of the advance reaching the lender.
Repayment deadlineBefore the cycle ends and before the group’s share-out.
Early repaymentAllowed: the group may repay the whole balance at any time.
Repayment accountOne PAMOJA account per lender, paid with a control number.
Unused fundsReturned to TAMISEMI.
Repayments to TAMISEMIThe lender passes collected repayments, with interest, back to TAMISEMI. Timing still to be agreed (Q17).
Closing a loanThe lender confirms “fully repaid” before the share-out.
RecoveryThe lender collects and recovers.
CMG incentivesReward on-time and early full repayment.

      Glossary

      Advance
      Money TAMISEMI sends a lender ahead of lending, based on demand estimates. The lender passes the repayments it collects, with interest, back to TAMISEMI, and returns any funds it does not lend.
      Actor journey map
      A loop of the steps one party is responsible for, in order, showing who else each step affects.
      Amortisation schedule
      The repayment schedule: how much is due and when, until the loan is repaid. Called “repayment schedule” in this chart.
      Annual cycle
      The group’s savings year, with fixed start and end dates. Profits are shared out at the end.
      Appraisal
      The lender’s check of an application before deciding whether to lend, and how much.
      Bullet payment
      Repaying the whole loan in one payment, instead of instalments.
      Closed (fully repaid)
      A loan status: the lender has confirmed the loan is fully repaid.
      CMG
      Community Microfinance Group: a savings group whose members save and lend to each other.
      Constitution
      The group’s written rules for how it runs.
      Control number
      A reference number the lender issues so each repayment can be matched to the right loan.
      Days past due
      How many days a payment is late.
      Defaulted
      A loan status: the loan was not repaid as agreed. The exact trigger is still open (Q12).
      Demand estimate
      An estimate of how much eligible CMGs are likely to borrow. TAMISEMI uses it to size each lender’s advance.
      Discount rate
      The rate at which PAMOJA funds are lent to the lender, agreed in the lending agreement.
      Eligible CMG
      A registered CMG that meets the eligibility criteria, so the LGA can assign it lenders. The criteria are still to be agreed (Q16).
      FI
      Financial Institution. Called “Lender” throughout this chart.
      Gateway (IN / OUT)
      The points where a group enters the PAMOJA loan journey (when it applies to its lender) and leaves it (when its loan closes, its cycle ends with no loan, or a defaulted loan is fully settled).
      Inside (the loan journey)
      Working term for the steps that make up the PAMOJA loan itself, from application to leaving. Shown inside the green frame.
      How often (tag)
      A tag showing when a step really happens: continuously, once then as needed, each instalment, live, or on a regular schedule (such as quarterly). CMG steps and steps 15, 17 and 22 carry no tag. Step numbers show a typical order for one group, not a timeline.
      Instalment
      One of several regular repayments.
      Interest rate ceiling
      The highest interest rate a lender may charge a CMG, set by TAMISEMI.
      Key disclosures
      A short, simple summary given with every offer: loan amount, tenure, interest rate or fee, payment schedule, how to pay, what counts as a delay, when the loan is in default, the consequences of default, and loan utilisation restrictions.
      Lender
      A bank or microfinance institution selected by TAMISEMI to lend PAMOJA funds to CMGs.
      LGA
      Local Government Authority: the district or town council.
      Loan contract
      The written agreement between the group and the lender, with the loan terms and key disclosures. The group signs it after accepting the offer.
      Loan utilisation restrictions
      Rules on what the loan may and may not be used for.
      Offer
      The lender’s approval sent to the group: a loan contract with terms, and key disclosures.
      Outside
      Working term for steps that are not part of the loan journey itself: pre-loan and post-loan.
      PAMOJA
      The World Bank-supported project (P178813) that funds these loans.
      Post-loan
      Outside steps after the loan journey: lenders passing repayments back to TAMISEMI; TAMISEMI’s quarterly payments to lenders, default settlements, LGA service fees and CMG incentives.
      Pre-loan
      Outside steps before the loan journey: the group forms, sets itself up and registers; TAMISEMI selects lenders and signs agreements; councils assign 1 to 2 lenders to each eligible CMG; invitations and advances go out.
      Quorum
      The minimum number of members who must attend for a meeting to take decisions.
      Risk sharing
      An agreed formula for how TAMISEMI and the lender share losses on loans that default.
      Service fee
      A payment from TAMISEMI to an LGA for its role, linked to eligible CMGs assigned lenders, loans and repayments.
      Share-out
      When the group divides its savings and profits among members at the end of the cycle.
      TAMISEMI
      The President’s Office – Regional Administration and Local Government (also known as PO-RALG), which runs PAMOJA nationally.
      Written off
      A loan status: a defaulted loan the lender removes from its active books.
      Tenure
      How long the loan runs, from payout to the final repayment.
      WEZESHA
      The existing system LGAs use to register groups.

      Version 3.13 (draft for review), 28 September 2026. The lender’s offer (step 15) now comes with a loan contract and simplified key disclosures: loan amount, tenure, interest rate or fee, payment schedule, how to pay, what counts as a delay, when the loan is in default, consequences of default, and loan utilisation restrictions. Accepting (step 16) means the group has understood the offer, including its terms and disclosures. New step 17 “Sign the loan contract with the lender”, before the loan is paid out. New open question Q18 on how long a group that defaults is not eligible for a new loan. Later steps move up by one, so there are 28 steps. Key rules and glossary updated.
      Version 3.12, 27 September 2026. New step 23 “Pass repayments back to TAMISEMI” (Lender, post-loan): the lender passes collected repayments, with interest, back to TAMISEMI and returns unused funds (money map flows 4 and 5). Open question Q17 now links to it. Old steps 23 to 26 are now 24 to 27, so there are 27 steps. The step also appears in the system map, the lender’s actor journey map and the walkthrough.
      Version 3.11, 27 September 2026. Changes from 3.10, from review comments: (1) new actor journey maps: one loop for each party (TAMISEMI, LGA, Lender, CMG) showing the steps it is responsible for and who else each step affects; the detail panel also shows this. (2) Step 11 “Form eligible cohorts” removed. Step 11 is now “Assign 1–2 TAMISEMI designated lenders to all eligible registered CMGs, who can then apply for loans”, with a new open question (Q16) on the eligibility criteria, such as age and savings. (3) Cohorts and pools are gone throughout: step 12 invitations are triggered by the lender assignment; step 13 advances are based on demand estimates; a CMG can apply to either of its 1 to 2 assigned lenders; the LGA service fee follows eligible CMGs assigned lenders; stage 4, the key rules, the money map, open questions and glossary are updated to match. (4) Zone headings are now “Pre-loan · Setting up” and “Post-loan · Settlement”. (5) The actor journey maps show no step numbers; step numbers stay in the main flow. (6) Step numbers are now described as a typical order for one group, not a timeline. Most steps have a ‘how often’ tag (none on CMG steps or steps 15, 17 and 22), shown in the flowchart, system map, actor journey maps and walkthrough panel, with a legend under the flowchart key. Steps 23 to 26 (TAMISEMI’s payments) are all quarterly; steps 11 and 12 are continuous. (8) The money map is redrawn as rings: CMGs at the centre, the LGA (30%) and lender (70%) around them, and TAMISEMI as the outer arc. It shows every fund flow, including two separate flows back to TAMISEMI (repayments collected with interest, flow 4; unused funds, flow 5) and both routes for CMG incentives: 8A “Incentives (direct)” and 8B “Incentives (via Lender)”. New open question Q17 on when and how repayments go back to TAMISEMI.
      Version 3.10, 27 September 2026. Step 21 “Reach out on delays” (LGA outreach at 15 days past due) removed, with its arrows, the outreach flow (9) on the money map, the outreach part of the Recovery rule, and settled question Q11. Steps 22 to 28 are now 21 to 27.
      Version 3.9, 27 September 2026. Outside zones renamed: “Programme set-up” is now “Pre-loan”, and “Programme settlement” is now “Post-loan”, in the map, flowchart, legend and glossary. Content unchanged.
      Version 3.8, 27 September 2026. Outside zones made more muted in the system map and the flowchart: neutral grey backgrounds, softer stakeholder colours, lighter text. Content unchanged.
      Version 3.7, 27 September 2026. New step 26 “Settle defaults with lenders”: TAMISEMI pays its risk share on defaulted loans. The LGA service fee and CMG incentives are now steps 27 and 28. No other changes.
      Version 3.6, 27 September 2026. Steps 13 and 14 swapped: “Invite applications” is now step 13 and “Release advances to lenders” is step 14. Stage 5 renamed to match. No other changes.
      Version 3.5, 27 September 2026. Step 14 “Invite applications” is now shown as triggered by the pool allocation (step 12), with TAMISEMI notified when invitations go out. No other changes.
      Version 3.4, 27 September 2026. Step 12 renamed “Allocate the CMG cohort pool across lenders designated to the council by TAMISEMI”, in both the system map and the flowchart. No other changes.

      Version 3.2, 27 September 2026. “Set the annual cycle” and “Agree the group’s rules” moved up to become steps 2 and 3, so the group sets itself up before submitting documents. Old steps 2, 3 and 5 are now 4, 5 and 6; stages 1 and 2 renamed to match. All other steps unchanged.

Step content and numbering are unchanged from 3.0.
      Version 3.0, 27 September 2026. Adds “inside / outside” boundaries (answers B1–B6): the loan journey runs from the group’s application (▶ IN, step 15) to leaving (◀ OUT, step 24); everything else is outside. Adds the one-page system map, stronger stakeholder colours, and a switch to hide outside steps. Steps reordered: shared status and reports now come before leaving, so steps 22–24 are renumbered; the lender’s invitation moved into stage 5.
      Version 2.2, 27 September 2026. Layout only: the detail panel now opens on the right on laptop and tablet screens, with the flowchart on the left. Content is unchanged from 2.1.
      Version 2.1, 27 September 2026. Changes from 2.0: answers to Q1, Q3, Q4, Q7, Q10, Q11, Q13 and Q15 applied; options added to the seven questions still open; three final loan statuses added; unused-advance return added to the money map.
      Version 2.0, 27 September 2026. A new flow built from the updated PAMOJA journey notes. It replaces the earlier CMG Loan Tracker beta flow (version 1), which is kept separately. Main changes from version 1: group size is now 10 to 50; registration goes through WEZESHA; TAMISEMI assigns 2 to 3 lenders per council; LGAs build eligible pools; advances, discounts, incentives and fees are added; repayment is by control number into one account per lender. “Lender” is used throughout for the bank or microfinance institution (FI).
